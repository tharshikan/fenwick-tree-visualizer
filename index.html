    The trade-off
    
      
    
        
    updateprefix query
    
        
          
    Plain arrayO(1)O(n)
    
          
    Prefix-sum arrayO(n)O(1)

    The other two structures each have one O(n) operation, so a mix of queries and updates costs them O(n) per operation. Fenwick has no slow operation. Both of its loops run once per bit of the index.
